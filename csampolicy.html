Child Safety Standards Policy | बाल सुरक्षा नीति

1. Zero Tolerance Statement | शून्य सहनशीलता नीति

Fin Rein Inc. ("Company", "we", "us"), established in Kanpur & Lucknow, UP, India, maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), and any form of child endangerment across all features of the PALZEE application.

फिन रेन इंक. PALZEE ऐप पर बाल यौन शोषण सामग्री (CSAM) और बाल उत्पीड़न के खिलाफ सख्त शून्य-सहनशीलता नीति अपनाता है।

2. Technical Prevention & Moderation | सुरक्षा एवं मॉडरेशन

We deploy robust multi-layered safety mechanisms on the PALZEE platform:

  • Automated Media Hash Matching: Uploaded media is scanned via Supabase Edge Functions against known CSAM hash registries before distribution.
  • Content Quarantine: Any flagged media is immediately blocked from transit and quarantined in encrypted isolation.
  • In-App Reporting: Users can flag inappropriate media or accounts 24/7 directly through the app interface.

3. Mandatory Law Enforcement Reporting | क़ानूनी रिपोर्टिंग

Upon detecting any CSAM/CSAE content or attempt, Fin Rein Inc. immediately executes mandatory legal reporting:

  • Submission of report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Filing of cybercrime report with the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C / NCRP, MHA India) and local police authorities in Kanpur & Lucknow, UP.
  • Preservation of forensic evidence, IP addresses, device identifiers, and metadata for criminal prosecution.

4. Account Termination & Blacklisting | खाता निलंबन

Any account associated with CSAM/CSAE violations will face immediate permanent account termination, hardware device bans, and IP blacklisting.

5. Governing Law and Jurisdiction | शासी कानून

This Child Safety Standards Policy is governed by the laws of India, and any legal proceedings shall fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Kanpur & Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.
