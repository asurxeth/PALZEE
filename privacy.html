2. Collection of Information | एकत्र की जाने वाली जानकारी

We collect only the essential data required to deliver high-speed, local-first micro-vlogging experiences:

  • Account Credentials: Email address, username, and authentication data managed securely via Supabase Auth (io.supabase.pals).
  • Media Content: Daily vlogs, photos, keyframe snapshots (JPEG), and video clips (.mp4) captured using CameraX & ExoPlayer.
  • Device Permissions: Camera, Microphone (Audio), Media Storage, Push Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), and Background Data Sync.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for instant notifications.

3. Local-First Caching & Media Retention | मीडिया आर्किटेक्चर एवं कैशिंग

PALZEE utilizes a zero-latency local-first media processing pipeline:

  • State-Latch Keyframes: A high-speed worker generates a static JPEG keyframe using MediaMetadataRetriever at timeUs = 0 to render instant previews.
  • Algorithmic Garbage Collector (GC): Temporary files and preview caches in Context.cacheDir are automatically cleaned up on app launch and shutdown, keeping device storage overhead minimal.

4. Data Sharing & Security | डेटा साझाकरण एवं सुरक्षा

Your media content is shared strictly with your designated circle of friends ("My Pals"). We do not sell or rent personal information or media files to any third party. Data in transit and at rest is protected using industry-standard encryption on Supabase PostgreSQL and Edge CDN infrastructure.

5. Child Safety & CSAM Zero Tolerance | बाल सुरक्षा नीति

PALZEE enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE). For detailed safety procedures and legal reporting guidelines, please refer to our Child Safety Standards Policy.
